
# E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard

##  Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze the sales performance of a retail store selling a wide range of products, including electronics, footwear, clothing, home appliances, accessories, kitchenware, bags, and personal care products.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into interactive visualizations and business insights, enabling users to analyze **sales, profit, quantity sold, discounts, orders, products, customers, and geographical performance**.

##  Project Objectives

The dashboard was designed to answer key business questions such as:

- Identify the **Top 5 and Bottom 5 products** based on Sales, Profit, and Quantity Sold.
- Analyze sales trends across **daily, monthly, quarterly, and yearly** periods.
- Understand the relationship between **Sales and Profit**.
- Compare **Sales, Profit, and Quantity Sold between two user-selected time periods**.
- Analyze the **average discount** offered across different discount categories.
- Track the **total number of orders**.
- Analyze sales and other metrics using filters such as **Product, Date, Customer ID, and Promotion Category**.
- Analyze **sales performance across different cities**.

##  Data Model

The project uses a structured data model consisting of:

- **Dim Customers** – Customer ID, name, city, state, pincode, email and phone details.
- **Dim Product** – Product ID, product name, product line and price per unit.
- **Dim Promotion** – Promotion details, advertisement type, coupon code, discount type and percentage.
- **Fact Table** – Transaction-level information including date, customer, product, units sold, sales, discounts, net sales, profit and order ID.
- **Date Tables** – Used for time-based analysis and trend comparison.

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Overview Dashboard
Provides a high-level view of key business metrics such as:

- Net Sales
- Total Profit
- Quantity Sold
- Number of Orders

### 2. Top/Bottom 5 Product Analysis
Analyzes products based on:

- Sales
- Profit
- Quantity Sold

This helps identify products with strong and weak performance.

### 3. Sales & Time Analysis
Enables analysis of sales performance over different time periods, including:

- Daily
- Monthly
- Quarterly
- Yearly

### 4. Period Comparison
Allows users to select two different time periods and compare:

- Sales
- Profit
- Quantity Sold

### 5. Discount Analysis
Analyzes discounts offered across different discount categories and examines their effect on sales performance.

### 6. Geographical Analysis
Analyzes sales performance across different **cities**, helping identify geographical variations in sales.

### 7. Interactive Filtering
The dashboard provides interactive filtering based on dimensions such as:

- Product
- Date
- Customer ID
- Promotion Category
